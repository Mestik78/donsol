# Game Rules
Donsol is a card dungeon crawler played with a standard (French suited) deck. In the table there's the deck, the current room (a collection of 4 cards), the current equipment/artifacts and the discard deck.
The player has HP and a token for skipping the current room.
Once you interact with a card (and it's not currently equipped) it will be placed on the discard deck.
The rules differ a bit between the different game modes listed here.
## Original (Hundred Rabbits)
- When the game starts the player has 21 HP, no shield equipped and the room skip token.
- Each round 4 cards are drawn from the deck and placed in the table, they are the new room you have just entered.
- If the player has the skip token they can choose to consume it and skip the room without interacting with anything on it.
- If the player chooses to play the room they have to interact with every card on it in the order they prefer.
    - ♦ are shields with strength equal to the card number (face cards and A have strength 11). They can either be ignored or be equipped as the players current shield, replacing the current one if the player already had shield.
    - ♥ are health potions. When used they will increase the current player health as much as the card number says (face cards and A heal 11HP). The health will never increase above 21HP.
    - ♠ and ♣ are enemies. Interacting with them will start a fight. You will recibe as much damage as the power of the enemy minus the current shield strength. If the power of the enemy is greater or equal to the current shield strength, the shield will break after the battle. The power of an enemy is equal to its card number (J=11, Q=13, K=15, A=17 and Joker=21).
- Once the dungeon deck runs out and the player finishes the last room they win the game.
